# Tickmark privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Tickmark is a Forge app that runs on Atlassian's platform inside your Jira site.

## What the app reads
As the person using it and with that person's permissions: the work items in a project that are set up as controls or
are evidence requests, with their summary, assignee, status, due date, attachment details (file name, upload time and
uploader) and the app's two issue properties; the person's account id (to assign sample controls to them); and the
site's address (for links). The app makes no calls outside your Atlassian site.

## What the app writes
Only when a person asks for it: a control's setup (stored as an issue property named tickmark), evidence request work
items with their property (tickmark-request) and a link to the control, and the sample controls with their sample
files (Load sample controls). Everything it writes stays in your Jira site.

## What the app stores
Nothing outside Jira. The developer does not collect, receive or sell personal data. Atlassian's platform keeps its
usual operational logs for Forge apps; see Atlassian's privacy policy.

## Contact
Questions and requests: the issues page of the app's public code repository.
